About Auromaa Associates

Auromaa Associates is an intellectual property law firm based in Kolkata, advising clients across India and abroad. For 18 years, our IP lawyers have helped innovators, creators and businesses secure and defend their intellectual property rights.

Core Practice

A Focused IPR Firm

Intellectual property is all we do. We handle trademarks, patents, copyright, designs, geographical indications, plant varieties and semiconductor layouts, and advise on IP in licensing, transactions and insolvency.

From the first search to enforcement, clients get the full range of IPR services from one firm. Whether securing statutory protection before registry benches or pursuing contentious infringement proceedings before High Courts, our focus remains dedicated solely to intellectual property law.
