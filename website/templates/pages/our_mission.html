Purpose & Direction

Our Mission

At Auromaa Associates, our mission is to help people and businesses in India and overseas protect, manage and enforce their intellectual property. We do it by listening closely, understanding what each client wants to achieve, and delivering IPR services that move them towards that goal.

Future Outlook

Where We Are Headed

We aim to be a complete IPR firm for our clients: one place for searches, filings, prosecution, licensing, audits and enforcement. We will keep growing our team and expertise in India while serving clients in our chosen international markets.
