About the Firm

An IPR Firm Built Around Protecting What You Create

Auromaa Associates is a full-service intellectual property law firm based in India. For 18 years, our team has helped businesses, start-ups, creators, researchers and institutions secure and enforce their intellectual property in India and overseas.

We work as one team of senior partners and younger IP lawyers, so every matter gets both experience and close attention. Our practice rests on four values: competence, confidentiality, commitment and courtesy.
